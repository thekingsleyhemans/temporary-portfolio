My Process

01. Understand

I get to know the project, its story, its audience, and what it needs to communicate. Before thinking about layouts or interactions, I focus on understanding the idea behind the work.

02. Design

I explore visual systems, typography, composition, and interaction to find a direction that feels right for the project. Every decision should support the story rather than follow a trend.

03. Development

I bring the design to life through code, developing the interactions, motion, and underlying systems that make the experience work. Design and development happen together, not as disconnected steps.

04. Testing & Launch

I test, adjust, and refine the experience across devices and interactions. From the smallest visual detail to the overall flow, I aim for a final result that feels intentional, responsive, and complete.
